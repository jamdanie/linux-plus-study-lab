# Which two drives can the rack lose?

Use four scraps of paper or cover columns on screen. This model uses two mirrored pairs. Read across one row to assemble a complete record. Each letter stands for a different block.

| Stripe | Bay 1 · pair A | Bay 2 · pair A | Bay 3 · pair B | Bay 4 · pair B |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | A | A | B | B |
| Second | C | C | D | D |

1. Cover bay 2. Identify one surviving location for each of A, B, C and D.
2. Keep bay 2 covered and cover bay 3. Can you still assemble both records?
3. Uncover bay 3 and cover bay 1 instead. Count which distinct blocks remain.

<details><summary>Check the surviving blocks</summary>

In round one, bay 1 has A and C. Either member of pair B has B and D. In round two, bays 1 and 4 still supply all four blocks. In round three, bays 3 and 4 both have B and D. Neither surviving bay has A or C. The failure count stayed at two, but the pair that lost both members changed the outcome.

</details>
