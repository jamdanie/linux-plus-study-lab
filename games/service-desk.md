# Play the service desk shift

A fictional rack monitor stopped sending reports after maintenance. Your goal is to close three tickets without guessing which part failed. Play in GitHub on a phone or computer. Each round starts with a different host. Choose your first check before opening the answer. Score one point for the check and one for explaining what its output establishes.

| Round | Ticket | What you need to decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [The reboot](service-reboot.md) | How can a service be running today and still need attention before the next boot? |
| 2 | [The failed collector](service-failure.md) | Which event actually explains the failure? |
| 3 | [The quiet monitor](service-output.md) | What evidence would let you tell the user the job works again? |

For a portfolio entry, pick the round you initially missed. Save the symptom, the evidence that changed your mind, and one follow-up check. Label the case simulated. A reader can follow that decision even without your score.
